Across the middle is the lifecycle. Develop and iterate: Snowpark pivots the tall tag table per stand, XGBoost trains with a time-based split, and every run lands in Experiment Tracking under STUCK_PIPE_EXPERIMENT. Manage: the STAND_DRILLING_FEATURES feature view on the RIG_STAND entity, and the Model Registry holding STUCK_PIPE_MODEL and RIG_STATE_CLASSIFIER. The dashed boxes, ML Jobs for scheduled retraining and ML Observability for drift and feature importance, are not built today; they are the natural next steps on the same objects. Training runs on Container Runtime, SQL runs on the HOL_PTEN_WH adaptive warehouse, and the outputs feed the app, the agent's ScoreRigNow tool and the semantic view.
ML lifecycle

One governed path from tag data to a live risk score

Here is the same story as a path you will walk in the next forty minutes. Raw tag values become stand-level features in the Feature Store. Training logs to STUCK_PIPE_EXPERIMENT and the winning model is registered as STUCK_PIPE_MODEL version V1. From the registry it goes two ways: batch scoring in SQL writes every stand into PREDICTIONS.STUCK_PIPE_SCORES, which the semantic view exposes to the agent, and the same model is deployed as STUCK_PIPE_SVC for interactive scoring through the ScoreRigNow tool. The app calls the agent, and every thumbs up or down lands next to the trace. Every arrow stays inside one account and one role, which is why there is no copy step to govern.

Before we build the agent, here is where it sits. DSA_AGENT · after B4

One agent, three kinds of tools

Solid boxes are built in the lab. Dashed boxes are next steps.

With B4 done, this is the whole agent. People reach DSA_AGENT through the REST API, which is how the PTEN+ app calls it, or through the Snowsight playground; MCP and agent-to-agent access are next steps on the same object. The agent plans, calls tools, reflects on the results and is monitored natively. It now has three kinds of tools: Cortex Search over your documents for the unstructured questions, Cortex Analyst over the semantic view for scores, MSE, connection times, drag and NPT, and custom procedures for anything else, here GetTagWindow for selective tag retrieval and ScoreRigNow calling the model service. Adding a capability means adding a tool, not rebuilding the agent.
