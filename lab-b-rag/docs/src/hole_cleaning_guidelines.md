# Why hole cleaning matters

Cuttings that are not removed from the annulus settle on the low side of deviated holes and form beds. Beds raise torque and drag and are a leading cause of pack-off and stuck pipe.

# Indicators of poor hole cleaning

- Torque and drag above the modeled trend.
- Overpull when picking up off bottom.
- Rising standpipe pressure at constant flow rate.
- Cuttings volume at the shakers lower than expected for the current ROP.

# Parameter guidelines (illustrative)

| Hole section | Flow rate (gpm) | RPM | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12-1/4 in. vertical | 800 to 950 | 80 to 120 | Pump sweeps every 3 to 4 stands |
| 8-3/4 in. curve | 550 to 650 | 60 to 100 | Limit ROP if torque trends up |
| 8-3/4 in. lateral | 550 to 650 | 120 to 160 | High RPM aids cuttings transport |

# Controlled ROP

When hole cleaning indicators appear, reduce ROP until torque and SPP stabilize. Drilling faster than the hole can be cleaned trades short-term footage for a higher chance of NPT.

# Circulating before trips and connections

Circulate bottoms up at least once before tripping out of a deviated section. In high-risk intervals, circulate and rotate for the time of one bottoms up before each connection.
